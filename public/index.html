Our story

Pizza for the whole barkada.

It all began in a small kitchen in Bagong Silang, Caloocan, where Nanay Lirio wanted just one thing: a table full of the people she loved. Every Sunday, cousins, neighbors and friends squeezed in around it, and nobody ever left hungry.

The first pizza was for her youngest child's birthday. Money was tight, so she stretched the dough by hand, spread a simple tomato sauce and covered it with cheese. It was not perfect, but the whole house crowded around it, laughing, and it was gone in minutes.

"Food tastes better when it is shared." That was her rule, and it still is ours.

Word spread down the street. Neighbors began knocking on the gate: "Nay, can we order one for tonight?" Soon a handwritten menu was taped to the fence, and the family kitchen became Pizza Haus at 176 Bagong Silang.

Today we still make our dough fresh every morning and bake every pizza to order, just the way she did. We think pizza is best on a birthday, after a long day, or on an ordinary Sunday when everyone is finally home. When you order from us, you are not just a customer. You are part of the barkada.

Thank you for letting us sit at your table.
